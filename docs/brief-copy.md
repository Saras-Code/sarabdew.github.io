  Label: Cover Story
  Headline: How a math paper turned into a dating app
  Subhead: Co-Founder & CEO, Posy Social Inc. · Feb 2025 to present · Seattle
  Body (drop cap on the first letter):
  "Posy is a curated matchmaking app for real-life dates, and it actually started as a research project. In my first year at UW, I co-wrote a paper in the math department that adapted Dijkstra's shortest-path algorithm to model compatibility, and once the model held up in simulation, my co-founder Nicholas Claeys and I decided to build the real thing.
  Before writing a single line of code, we surveyed 35 students about how they actually meet people and then ran a two-month closed beta. I wrote the entire matching service along with the recommendation system that picks where each pair goes on their first date, and we shipped a three-tier app (React Native, Firebase, Python) to the App Store.
  Since launching, Posy has grown to 220+ users and 50 matches at UW without spending a dollar on ads, picked up 44,000+ views online, been featured in The Daily, and placed in the top 10% of Y Combinator's Fall 2026 applicants. Our first in-person events are booked with Fremont Brewing and The Good Bar, so stay tuned!"
